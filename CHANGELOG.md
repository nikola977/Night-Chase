# Changelog

All notable changes to **Night Chase** (project “Endlose Welt”). Versions before 1.0 are roughly
summarized from the Git history.

## 1.0.0 – 17.11.2026

### New
- **Loading screen** with rotating images from the game, a real loading status (roads, terrain, distant view,
  car) and tips about keys and features.
- **Smooth animations** in the pause menu, garage, map, photo mode and HUD; messages stack without
  overlapping.
- **Key hints**: When stationary, at rest stops, at viewpoints and on the dyno, the matching keys are shown
  at the bottom of the screen (can be turned off under Settings > Game).
- **Cockpit and first-person view** (Tab) with a turning steering wheel, instrument cluster and rear-view mirrors.
- **Rewind** (hold Backspace, up to 10 seconds, with traffic) and **2WD mode** for the M5 (U).
- **Right of way at junctions**: signs, stop lines, AI cars wait; traffic follows the time of day with rush hour
  and traffic jams.
- **Long tunnels** with emergency niches, escape route signs, fans, breakdown bays and names at the portal.
- **Trees in three levels of detail** with smooth transitions, groups of trees, clearings, reeds and stones on the shore.
- Raindrops, snow and ice on the car, soft shadow under all cars.
- Indicators in the lamp glass, welcome light at engine start, headlights with a light-dark cutoff.
- **“What’s new”** window after every update, version number in the pause menu.

### Improved
- Camera with spring, the body pitches and rolls, collisions with sparks and a scraping sound.
- Engine sound at 48 kHz with limiter, quieter during announcements, reverb matching the surroundings.
- Crests and dips rounded off: even at 250 km/h the car no longer takes off.
- Rock without visible repetition, grass edge on the shoulder, tire tracks and patches in the asphalt.
- Fewer stutters while loading more of the world, shaders are prepared during loading.
- Loading screen no longer stays stuck at 100 % when the garage opens, and loading has a timeout safety.

## 0.9 – 5.–13.11.2026

- New garage cars: BMW M4 CSL, Mercedes SL 65 AMG and C 63 AMG Black Series.
- Traffic with real models, rest stops with a gas station, car and truck parking spaces.
- Speed limits with signs, cruise control with distance control, headlight-flash races.
- Opening windows, license plates, puddles, autumn leaves.
- Workshop with a power dyno.
- Dams with reservoirs, waterfalls, arch and suspension bridges, life at the lakes.
- Moon phases, shooting stars, light pollution.
- Explorer map with fog and places collection, chill mode (autopilot with a film camera).
- Windows program “Night Chase” with logo and splash image, README in English.
- Automatic test run and world checker; many road and tunnel bugs fixed, reset at safe spots.

## 0.8 – 23.–27.10.2026
